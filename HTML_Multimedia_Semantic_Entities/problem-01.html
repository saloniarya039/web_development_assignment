Admissions 2026

Admission Announcement

Applications are now open for the 2026-27 academic session. Students who are interested in joining our university can check the available programs and apply online.

The last date for application may vary according to the program. Students should check the official admission notice before submitting the form.

Application Guidance

Before applying, keep your basic details and educational information ready. Check your eligibility carefully and fill the application form correctly.

Tip: Check all details before clicking the submit button.

Admission Steps

  1. Check the program and eligibility.
  2. Register on the admission portal.
  3. Fill in your personal details.
  4. Enter your educational qualification.
  5. Select your preferred program.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Pay the application fee.
  8. Wait for the merit list/admission update.

Important Information

Eligibility
Students must satisfy the minimum qualification required for the selected program.
Scholarship
Eligible students may receive scholarships according to university rules.
Merit List
The merit list is prepared according to the admission criteria.
Application Fee
The applicant has to pay the prescribed application fee while submitting the form.
