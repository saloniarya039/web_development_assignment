Destination Overview

Jaipur is famous for its forts, palaces, culture and colourful markets. It is a great place to experience Rajasthan.

Popular Attractions

  • Amber Fort
  • Hawa Mahal
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Jal Mahal
  • Albert Hall Museum
  • Nahargarh Fort
  • Birla Mandir

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Visit Amber Fort
  2. See Hawa Mahal
  3. Explore City Palace
  4. Visit Jantar Mantar
  5. Enjoy local food and shopping
