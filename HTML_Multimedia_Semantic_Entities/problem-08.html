Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account helps you keep money safely and earn interest.

How to Open an Account

  1. Choose a bank.
  2. Fill the application form.
  3. Submit required details.
  4. Complete verification.

Cards

Debit and Credit Cards

A debit card uses money from your bank account, while a credit card allows you to borrow money up to a limit.

Loans

Understanding Loans

Loans should be taken carefully. Always check the interest rate, charges and repayment period.

Interest Rate
Percentage charged on borrowed money.
EMI
Monthly payment made towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed.
Tenure
The time period for repaying a loan.

Important Notice

Do not borrow more than you can repay.

This website is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice.

Financial Tips

  • Make a monthly budget.
  • Save part of your income.
  • Compare charges before choosing a product.
  • Pay EMIs on time.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
"Save money today to prepare for tomorrow."
