Student Terma & Rules


General Rules

  • Student must bring student ID daily.
  • Student must obey the rules set by teacher and authorization
  • Zero tolerance for ragging, bullying, harassment, or violent behavior.
  • Complete prohibition of alcohol, tobacco, and illegal drugs anywhere on campus or in hostels.
  • No unauthorized carrying of weapons or dangerous items.


    • Attendance and Academics

  • Maintain a minimum attendance requirement, typically 75% in lectures and practicals, to sit for exams.
  • Submit valid medical certificates or leave applications for extended absences.
  • Uphold strict academic integrity by avoiding plagiarism or cheating on tests.


    • Facilities and Property

  • Maintain silence in designated areas like the library and reading rooms.
  • Handle college property with care and avoid defacing walls, desks, or equipment.
  • Keep the campus clean by using proper waste disposal bins.


    • Technology and Events

  • Restrict or silence the use of mobile phones during classes and formal academic zones.
  • Obtain prior approval from administration or the principal before organizing events, meetings, or collecting funds.
