GLA University


GLA University is a private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh and in Greater Noida. It is one of the leading institutions of higher learning in the country. It is good in providing quality education. The placement record is also impressive.

GLA University offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate courses in various disciplines such as Engineering, Management, Law, and Medicine. Campus Life

The campus life at GLA University is vibrant and engaging, with many activities and events organized throughout the year. There are various clubs and societies for students to join, including cultural, technical, and sports clubs. The University also has a well-equipped library, and hostels for students. The campus is well-maintained and provides a conducive environment for Learning and making new projects. The University also has a good sports facility for students to participate in various sports activities.
