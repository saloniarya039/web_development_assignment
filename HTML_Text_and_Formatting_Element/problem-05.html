Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the branch of science that studies matter, its properties, composition, and reactions.

Important Chemical Formulas:

  • Water: H2O
  • Carbon Dioxide: CO2
  • Oxygen: O2
  • Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4
  • Ammonia: NH3
  • Glucose: C6H12O6

    • Atom: The smallest unit of an element that retains its chemical properties.


    Physics

    Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, and forces.

    Important Scientific Expressions:

  • Area of a square = side2 or m2
  • Volume of a cube = side3 or m3
  • Speed of light ≈ 3 × 108 m/s
  • Acceleration = m/s2
    • Force: A push or pull that can change the motion of an object.


    Biology

    Biology is the study of living organisms and their life processes.

    • Cell – The basic structural and functional unit of life.
    • DNA – Carries genetic information.
    • Photosynthesis – Plants use sunlight to make food.
    • Human body temperature ≈ 37°C.
