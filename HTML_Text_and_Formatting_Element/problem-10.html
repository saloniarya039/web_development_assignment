About GLA University

GLA University is a private university located in Mathura(Uttar Pradesh) and Greater Noida. The university provides education in engineering, technology, management, pharmacy, and other academic fields. This also Provide the online courses also.

GLA University focuses on quality education, innovation, research, and practical learning. Students are encouraged to develop technical knowledge, communication skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities to prepare for their future careers.

The university also promotes creative thinking and continuous learning. Students are encouraged to explore new technologies and apply their knowledge to solve real-world problems.


Departments

The university has several academic departments that offer education in different areas of study. Each department has qualified faculty members, laboratories, classrooms, and learning resources.

Major departments include Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, and Management.

The Computer Science and Engineering Department focuses on programming, software development, databases, networking, and modern computing technologies. The Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning department focuses on intelligent systems, data analysis, machine learning, and emerging AI technologies.


Acadmemic Programs

The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and other professional programs. These programs have theoretical knowledge with practical learning so that students can prepare for higher studies and professional careers.

Undergraduate students study subjects such as mathematics, programming, communication skills, engineering fundamentals, and specialized technical subjects. For example, students may learn concepts involving area in m2 and volume in m3.


Campus Facilities

The university campus provides facilities designed to support both academic learning and student activities. Classrooms and laboratories provide students with an environment for theoretical and practical education.

Important facilities may include a library, computer laboratories, science laboratories, sports facilities, seminar halls, cafeterias, transportation services, and student activity areas.


Important Information

Students should regularly check official university announcements for information about examinations, assignments, academic schedules, holidays, seminars, and other important activities.

Students must submit assignments before the specified deadline. Late submissions may be subject to university rules or penalties.
