WELCOME TO CAREPOINT

Healthcare that puts patients first

CarePoint Clinic is a multi-specialty clinic providing routine consultations, follow-up care and specialist services in a comfortable and patient-friendly environment.

Our team focuses on clear communication, responsible healthcare and helping patients understand their available care options.

About Our Clinic

CarePoint Clinic brings several healthcare specialties together under one roof. Patients can request consultations, discuss routine health concerns with qualified professionals, and receive guidance about follow-up visits.

The clinic provides services by appointment and also maintains selected OPD consultation hours. Patients are encouraged to check the schedule before visiting.

"Good healthcare begins with listening carefully to the patient."

Patient Information & Resources

Before Your Visit

  • Carry a valid identification document if required.
  • Bring previous medical reports when relevant.
  • Arrive approximately 10 minutes before your appointment.
  • Keep your appointment confirmation available.
  • Inform reception if you need to change your appointment.

Common Clinic Terms

Consultation
A scheduled discussion with a healthcare professional regarding the patient's health concerns.
Follow-up
A later consultation used to review progress or continue an existing care plan.
OPD
Outpatient Department services where patients receive consultation without hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time requested by a patient to meet a healthcare professional.

How to Request an Appointment

  1. Select your preferred department.
  2. Select an available doctor.
  3. Choose your preferred date and time.
  4. Enter your basic contact information.
  5. Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
  6. Read and accept the appointment request consent.
  7. Submit the form and wait for confirmation from the clinic.
