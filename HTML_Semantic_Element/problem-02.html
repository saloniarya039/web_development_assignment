Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to understand your technical knowledge, communication skills and ability to work with a team.

  1. Application Submission
  2. Resume Screening
  3. Technical Round
  4. HR Round
  5. Final Selection
  6. Offer

Hiring Stages Explained

Screening
The recruitment team reviews your resume, skills, education and experience.
Technical Round
Candidates answer technical questions related to the position and may be given a small problem-solving task.
HR Round
The HR team discusses communication, career goals, availability and company culture.
Offer
Selected candidates receive the final offer and information about joining the company.

Recruiter Tips

  • Keep your resume short and clear.
  • Mention your important technical skills.
  • Add projects that you have actually completed.
  • Check your email and phone number before submitting.
  • Prepare basic technical and HR questions.
