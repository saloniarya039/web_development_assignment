Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a platform where students, graduates and job seekers can meet companies, colleges and training organizations.

The main objective is to provide career guidance, education options, internships and placement opportunities under one roof.

"Our aim is to help every participant make a better and informed career decision." — Event Organizer

Preparation Guide

Notice: Carry your college ID, updated resume and a notebook.

  • Prepare a simple and professional resume.
  • Research the organizations before attending.
  • Dress neatly and reach early.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Keep questions ready for recruiters.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Check the schedule.
  3. Visit organization stalls.
  4. Attend workshops and talks.
  5. Talk to recruiters.
  6. Apply for suitable opportunities.
