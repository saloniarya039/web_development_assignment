About

GLA University is a higher education institution that provides students with opportunities to learn through academic programs, practical activities, projects, and extracurricular activities.

The university focuses on providing a supportive learning environment where students can prepare themselves for future careers and professional life.

Programs

The university offers programs in areas such as Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, and other academic fields.

Students can choose programs according to their interests and career goals. The programs include both theoretical learning and practical exposure.

Campus Life

Campus life provides students with opportunities to participate in technical clubs, cultural activities, sports, competitions, workshops, and other events.
