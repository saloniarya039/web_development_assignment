College Holds Orientation Program for New Students

Freshers receive important information about academics, campus facilities and student activities

The college organized an orientation program for newly admitted students to help them understand their new academic environment. The program introduced freshers to their teachers, courses, college facilities and important academic activities. Students also received guidance about how to begin their college journey.

Venue of orintation

The orientation program was conducted at the college campus in Greater Noida, India . New students gathered in the Room No. 401 where faculty members welcomed them and explained various aspects of college life. Students also learned about classrooms, laboratories, the library and other facilities available on campus.

The orintation going to be held

The orientation program was held at the beginning of the new academic session in 5 August 2026. During the program, studentsn received information about their class schedules, subjects, attendance requirements and upcoming academic activities. The event helped students understand what they could expect during their first year.

The purpose to be held orientation

The main purpose of the orientation was to help freshers feel comfortable in their new surroundings and understand their academic responsibilities. The program also encouraged students to ask questions and actively participate in college activities. Faculty members explained that students can make the most of their college experience by attending classes regularly, learning new skills and taking part in different opportunities.
