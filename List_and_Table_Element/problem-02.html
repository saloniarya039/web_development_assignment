Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden, a family-friendly restaurant serving delicious Indian and international dishes made with fresh ingredients. Enjoy our signature dishes, special offers, refreshing beverages and warm hospitality.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled cottage cheese with onions and peppers.

    Vegetarian: A dish prepared without meat or fish.

  • Veg Spring Rolls - Crispy rolls filled with fresh vegetables.
  • Chicken Seekh Kebab - Spiced minced chicken grilled on skewers. Chef's Special

Main Course

  • Butter Paneer - Soft paneer cooked in a rich tomato and butter gravy. Bestseller
  • Veg Biryani - Fragrant basmati rice cooked with vegetables and aromatic spices. Vegan
  • Chicken Biryani - Traditional basmati rice cooked with tender chicken and spices. Chef's Special

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Soft milk-solid dumplings served with sugar syrup.
  • Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie served as a sweet treat. Bestseller
  • Fruit Ice Cream - Creamy ice cream with fresh seasonal fruits. Gluten-Free

Beverages

  • Mango Lassi - Fresh mango yogurt drink.
  • Masala Chai - Traditional Indian spiced tea.
  • Fresh Lime Soda - Refreshing lime-based drink.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Start with our Paneer Tikka.
  2. Enjoy our Chicken Biryani as the main course.
  3. Pair your meal with a refreshing Mango Lassi.
  4. Finish your dining experience with our Gulab Jamun.
