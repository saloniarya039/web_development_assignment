Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to our Annual College Fest 2026 — a three-day celebration of talent, creativity, technology, sports and entertainment.

Fest Introduction

The Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day inter-college event designed to provide students with opportunities to showcase their skills and creativity.

The fest includes cultural events, technical competitions, sports, quizzes, music, dance and various individual and team activities.

"A festival is not just about winning; it is about learning, participating, creating memories and celebrating talent."

Registration Process

Students must complete the registration process before participating in any competition.

Registration Deadline: 30 September 2026

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Choose your desired competition.
  3. Enter your name, college and contact details.
  4. Select your participation type.
  5. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.
  8. Report to the venue before the scheduled event time.

General Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report to the venue at least 30 minutes before their event.
  • Registration must be completed before the deadline.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of the coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • The decision of the judges will be considered final.
  • Students must maintain discipline and cleanliness throughout the fest.

Fest Terms and Definitions

Open Event
An event in which students from different colleges can participate.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
An event where a participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing and managing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition in which selected participants compete for the winning position.
