Academic Rules

Classroom Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students should arrive before the class begins.
  • Mobile phones should be kept on silent mode.
  • Students must maintain discipline in the classroom.
  • Assignments should be submitted before the deadline.
  • Students should respect faculty members and classmates.

Mandatory Rule: Attendance requirements must be fulfilled according to university academic regulations.

Important Academic Notice: Students must regularly check the academic portal for examination schedules, assignment deadlines and departmental announcements.
