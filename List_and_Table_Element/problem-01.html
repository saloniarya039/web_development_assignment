Admission Information Handbook

Admission Overview

Welcome to the GLA University Admission Handbook. This page provides important information about eligibility, application procedures, required documents, admission terms, fees, and contact details.

Students are advised to read all instructions carefully before submitting their application. "The Admission Office is committed to providing a transparent, fair, and student-friendly admission process."

Eligibility

Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements specified for their selected program.

Mandatory requirement: Applicants must have passed the qualifying examination required for their chosen course.

Application Deadline: 30 September 2026

Application Process

Follow these steps in the given sequence:

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Register using your email address and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the completed application form.
  7. Check the merit list and counselling schedule.
  8. Complete admission and fee payment after selection.

Required Documents

Keep the following documents ready before applying:

  • Class 10 Mark Sheet and Certificate
  • Class 12 Mark Sheet and Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate, if applicable
  • Government-issued Identity Proof
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Entrance Examination Score Card, if applicable
  • Category Certificate, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic and other requirements an applicant must satisfy to apply for a program.
Merit List
A list of applicants arranged according to their academic performance or admission criteria.
Counselling
The process through which eligible or selected students choose courses or seats according to the admission rules.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on academic performance or other specified criteria.
Registration
The process of creating an applicant account and submitting basic information before applying for admission.
